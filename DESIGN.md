# Astraified visual direction

The product feels like opening an illustrated atlas and stepping into its harbor. A large living world carries the visual identity. Controls borrow from field notebooks and expedition instruments, kept compact around the scene.

Palette: harbor blue #122e48, deep water #193e58, cloud white #f3f7fa, sea glass #81b6b2, lantern gold #f3c97e, signal coral #d77c67. Typography pairs a literary Georgia display with a rounded, readable system sans. No marketing hero before the activity: the first screen offers a mission, format selection, and an immediate route into play, with source creation one navigation choice away.

Scenes receive most of the visual detail. The UI uses quiet paper surfaces, generous text spacing, and a small number of purposeful borders. Responsive changes preserve the mission and experiment before secondary information. Motion follows world behavior and user actions; reduced-motion settings suppress ambient movement.

First increment: complete three-station harbor mission, experiment/prediction/transfer loop, two scene modes, local save, source notebook, and source-to-mission generation for supported circuit/routing lessons. Large-scale world generation and deployment remain later milestones in ASTRAIFIED_PLAN.md.

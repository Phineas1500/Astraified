# The Last Light at Bramble Bay — first episode

The first playable edition is a complete authored case using the adventure model in POINT_AND_CLICK_DIRECTION.md. The generator and earlier experiments remain available separately while the new mission establishes the quality bar.

## Presentation

Palette: ink #173844, sea #17686d, foam #d8efe9, butter #f4ce7d, coral #d9866a, twilight #657790. Fraunces display type supplies storybook character; Nunito is the readable dialogue and control face. Illustrated rooms occupy almost all the game view. Buttons and the inventory borrow from a travel case and painted harbor fittings. The visual emphasis belongs to the environment and characters, rather than information cards.

Layout: a compact application bar above a 16:9 scene; scene name and map/tool controls at its corners; location exits and contextual inspection text along its bottom; a tactile inventory beneath. Dialogue appears within the scene. Small screens keep the scene intact and allow controls and dialogue to grow below it. Keyboard users can focus every interactive object and terminal. Reduced motion suppresses ambient movement.

The comparison to the brief favored recognizable rooms, tools and expressive characters over realistic landscape rendering. Original vector puppets and collectible props remain separate from AI-generated raster environments, making object state changes unambiguous and keeping scientific diagrams deterministic.

## Fun and learning

The player has two leads after a short introduction. They create a portable lamp to investigate a dark recess, and combine a hook with a cord to retrieve an old repair card. Evidence changes the maintenance robot's mind and gains its practical help. Real circuit connectivity governs the repair. The handover introduces a shared feeder fault, requiring a new diagnosis. Returning a keepsake is optional and unlocks a small interactive reward. Playful reactions and the crab companion reward curiosity without grading every click.

Success, assistance and discovered evidence are recorded independently; completing this mission is not a claim of mastery. Sources and simplified electrical assumptions are available in the notebook. Broader source-to-adventure generation follows once this reference episode is tested with learners.
